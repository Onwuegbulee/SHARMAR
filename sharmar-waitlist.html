FAQ

Questions you may want to ask

 

What is Sharmar?

An escrow platform for buyers and sellers. Payment is held safely until the buyer confirms the item or service is as agreed.

Do both sides need the app?

Yes. Buyer and seller both join the order so terms, evidence and approvals are visible to each side.

Do you deliver the item?

No. You choose your own delivery, such as a park driver. Sharmar records the driver’s details so the trip is traceable.

What if something goes wrong?

Photos, order terms and delivery records form the evidence trail, and a person reviews disputes at launch.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be shared before launch. Waitlist members get early perks.
